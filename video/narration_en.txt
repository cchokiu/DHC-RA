District Health Centres: why they exist, in one minute
In 2017, the Policy Address announced a first pilot in Kwai Tsing
Press releases tracked each step: contract, 11 interim centres, the Blueprint
Officials say the system leans too heavily on treatment
Primary care was described as fragmented
The Blueprint gives DHCs a dual role: support doctors, act as a district hub
Hospitals, clinics and family doctors each have a role; DHCs prevent and coordinate
Today: 10 centres, 8 interim centres, over 100 service points
Inference: a platform linking services, not another clinic. Government sources only.
